Privacy Policy

Last updated September 2, 2026

riMind does not collect, transmit, or store any of your data on our servers. Everything you add to the app — dates, photos, gift ideas, contacts — is saved directly on your own device. We have no access to it, and it is never sent anywhere.

To provide its features, riMind asks for permission to access a few things on your device. Here's exactly what each one is for:

  • ◐
    Contacts Used only if you choose to import birthdays or dates from your contacts. This data is saved on your device and is never uploaded or shared.
  • ▢
    Camera Used only when you choose to take a photo to attach to a date, so it appears alongside that person's reminder.
  • ▨
    Photo Library Used only when you choose to select an existing photo to attach to a date. riMind does not scan or access any photos beyond the one you pick.

riMind uses your device's notification system to remind you of upcoming dates. These reminders are scheduled and delivered entirely on your device.

Because everything is saved only on your device, deleting the app permanently removes all of its data. We have no copy to delete, because we never had one.

If this policy changes in the future, the update will be reflected here with a new "last updated" date. If you have questions about your privacy, contact us at mdouche1233@gmail.com.
